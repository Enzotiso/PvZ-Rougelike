Phase 1:
You get a plot of land
different starting seeds
you can plant these in any order relation to each other
you get N amount of times you can progress the grow stage for these plants
depending on their orientation crossbreeding my result in new plant
when plants are full grown and harvested you get rewards depending on plant
any new cross bread plant harvested will give you seeds to plant that one & use in Phase 2
growth button presses can either be used or saved between rounds
When out of growths or when finished, button can be pressed to proceed to Phase 2
